# Design Style Prompt Add-Ons

Two-line snippets you can append to any base prompt to steer output toward each style.

### 1. Flat Design
Flat design style, solid color blocks with no gradients, shadows, or textures.
Clean geometric shapes, bold simple iconography, high-contrast 2D compositions.

### 2. Material Design
Material Design style, layered cards with subtle drop shadows and elevation.
Bold primary colors, grid-based responsive layout, smooth motion-inspired transitions.

### 3. Glassmorphism
Glassmorphism style, frosted-glass translucent panels with background blur.
Soft light borders, vibrant blurred backdrops, subtle transparency layering.

### 4. Claymorphism
Claymorphism style, soft puffy 3D shapes with rounded matte clay-like surfaces.
Pastel color palette, soft inner and outer shadows, tactile toy-like rendering.

### 5. Minimalism
Minimalist design style, extreme simplicity with abundant negative space.
Muted neutral palette, single focal element, no decorative ornamentation.

### 6. Maximalism
Maximalist design style, dense layered composition with clashing patterns.
Bold saturated colors, eclectic mixed textures, "more is more" visual excess.

### 7. Swiss / International Typographic Style
Swiss International Typographic style, strict grid-based layout with clean sans-serif type.
Asymmetric balance, red/black/white palette, objective functional composition.

### 8. Memphis Design
Memphis Design style, bold clashing colors with playful geometric shapes.
Squiggles, zigzags, and confetti patterns, 1980s postmodern energy.

### 9. Bauhaus
Bauhaus style, geometric forms with primary colors (red, yellow, blue).
Functionalist composition, clean sans-serif type, circle/square/triangle motifs.

### 10. Art Deco
Art Deco style, ornate geometric symmetry with luxurious metallic accents.
Gold and black palette, sunburst and chevron patterns, elegant 1920s glamour.

### 11. Art Nouveau
Art Nouveau style, organic flowing lines inspired by natural forms.
Botanical motifs, muted earthy palette, elegant hand-drawn ornamental detail.

### 12. Vaporwave
Vaporwave style, retro-futuristic aesthetic with glitch and pastel neon tones.
Roman busts, palm trees, grid horizons, pink/purple/cyan gradient palette.

### 13. Synthwave / Outrun
Synthwave/Outrun style, neon grid horizon with glowing sunset gradient sky.
Magenta and cyan neon glow, chrome text, retro 80s digital landscape.

### 14. Cyberpunk
Cyberpunk style, dystopian neon-lit urban environment with high-tech low-life contrast.
Dark moody palette with neon accents, holographic UI elements, gritty texture.

### 15. Y2K Design
Y2K design style, chrome and bubble-text with early-2000s tech optimism.
Iridescent gradients, translucent plastic textures, futuristic bubbly typography.

### 16. Grunge Design
Grunge design style, distressed torn textures with raw handmade layering.
Muted desaturated palette, ink splatter and scratches, rebellious DIY aesthetic.

### 17. Bento Grid
Bento grid style, compartmentalized card-based modular layout.
Clean rounded rectangles, balanced whitespace, organized information hierarchy.

### 18. Dark Mode Minimalism
Dark mode minimalist style, low-light interface with high-contrast elements.
Deep charcoal/black background, single accent color, clean sans-serif typography.

### 19. Retro-Futurism
Retro-futurism style, nostalgic vision of "the future" from a past era.
Chrome and pastel palette, atomic-age shapes, optimistic space-age motifs.

### 20. Anti-Design
Anti-design style, deliberately raw and rule-breaking visual composition.
Clashing fonts, unpolished layout, intentional visual dissonance and chaos.

### 21. Raw Web Brutalism
Raw web brutalism style, unstyled HTML aesthetic with default browser typography.
Harsh black-and-white palette, visible structure, deliberately anti-polish layout.

### 22. Corporate Memphis (Alegria)
Corporate Memphis style, flat illustrated humans with rounded simplified shapes.
Bright flat color palette, disproportionate limbs, friendly approachable tech illustration.

### 23. Isometric Design
Isometric design style, 3D-like objects rendered on a flat 2D grid.
Consistent 30-degree angles, clean vector shading, miniature diorama feel.

### 24. Aurora / Gradient Mesh Design
Aurora gradient mesh style, soft blurred multicolor gradient backgrounds.
Flowing organic color blends, dreamy atmospheric glow, smooth mesh transitions.

### 25. Kinetic Typography
Kinetic typography style, motion-driven text as the primary visual element.
Dynamic scale and rotation, rhythmic timing, bold expressive type animation.

### 26. Data Visualization Minimalism
Data visualization minimalist style, clean dashboard-first analytical layout.
Muted palette with single accent color, clear charts, function-first typography.

### 27. Organic / Blob Design
Organic blob design style, amorphous soft rounded shapes with fluid contours.
Pastel or gradient fills, smooth morphing forms, playful abstract composition.

### 28. Pixel Art / 8-bit Design
Pixel art / 8-bit style, retro low-resolution blocky visuals.
Limited color palette, visible pixel grid, nostalgic video-game aesthetic.

### 29. Collage / Scrapbook Style
Collage/scrapbook style, layered cut-paper textures with mixed media elements.
Overlapping torn edges, handwritten notes, tape and stickers, DIY zine aesthetic.

### 30. Editorial / Magazine Style
Editorial magazine style, large expressive typography with asymmetric grid layout.
High-contrast imagery, generous whitespace, sophisticated print-inspired composition.

### 31. Neo-Memphis
Neo-Memphis style, modern revival of 80s Memphis design with refined palette.
Bold geometric shapes, softened color saturation, contemporary playful composition.

### 32. Frutiger Aero
Frutiger Aero style, glossy nature-tech aesthetic with optimistic 2000s sheen.
Blue sky and green foliage motifs, light reflections, smooth glassy surfaces.

### 33. Dark Academia (visual)
Dark Academia style, moody scholarly aesthetic with vintage academic references.
Deep browns and forest greens, serif typography, candlelit library atmosphere.

### 34. Cottagecore (visual)
Cottagecore style, pastoral nostalgic aesthetic with soft romanticized rural charm.
Muted florals and linen textures, warm natural light, handcrafted cozy details.

### 35. Solarpunk
Solarpunk style, eco-futurist aesthetic blending greenery with clean technology.
Lush vegetation integrated into architecture, warm sunlight, optimistic sustainable design.

### 36. Blackletter / Gothic Revival
Blackletter/Gothic Revival style, medieval-inspired ornate typographic detailing.
Dark dramatic palette, sharp angular letterforms, illuminated manuscript influence.

### 37. Op Art (Optical Art)
Op Art style, illusion-based geometric patterns creating visual movement.
High-contrast black-and-white or complementary colors, repeating hypnotic patterns.

### 38. Constructivism
Constructivism style, bold geometric propaganda-inspired composition.
Red/black/white palette, diagonal dynamic lines, industrial Soviet-era typography.

### 39. De Stijl (Neoplasticism)
De Stijl style, primary color blocks with strict black grid lines.
Horizontal/vertical composition only, Mondrian-inspired abstract balance.

### 40. Psychedelic Design
Psychedelic design style, swirling organic patterns with vibrant clashing colors.
Warped typography, kaleidoscopic visuals, 1960s-70s counterculture energy.

### 41. Hand-drawn / Sketch Style
Hand-drawn sketch style, imperfect organic linework with visible pencil or ink texture.
Loose expressive strokes, muted or monochrome palette, authentic illustrated feel.

### 42. 3D Render Realism
3D render realism style, hyperreal CGI rendering with physically accurate lighting.
Soft shadows and reflections, realistic materials and textures, studio-quality render.

### 43. Liquid / Fluid Design
Liquid/fluid design style, morphing blob-like shapes with smooth animated transitions.
Glossy translucent surfaces, vibrant gradient fills, continuous flowing motion.

### 44. Monochrome / Duotone Design
Monochrome/duotone style, limited two-tone color palette throughout composition.
High contrast tonal range, unified color mood, minimal but striking simplicity.

### 45. Retro Web 1.0 Revival
Retro Web 1.0 style, nostalgic early-internet aesthetic with visible table layouts.
Tiled backgrounds, marquee text, garish gradients, GeoCities-era charm.

### 46. Editorial Brutalism
Editorial brutalism style, raw brutalist typography paired with magazine-style layout.
Oversized bold type, harsh grid breaks, high-contrast black-and-white palette.

### 47. Soft UI
Soft UI style, subtle embossed shadows with muted pastel color palette.
Rounded smooth surfaces, gentle depth, calm approachable interface feel.

### 48. Techno / Blueprint Style
Techno/blueprint style, schematic engineering-drawing aesthetic with precise linework.
Blue-and-white palette, grid overlays, technical annotations and measurement marks.

### 49. Ligne Claire (Clear Line)
Ligne Claire style, uniform line weight with flat solid color fills.
No cross-hatching or gradients, clean comic-book illustration, bold outlined forms.

### 50. Big Type / Anti-Grid Design
Big type/anti-grid style, oversized typography breaking traditional grid constraints.
Overlapping bold text, asymmetric unconventional layout, high-impact visual hierarchy.

### 51. Neo-Brutalist
Neo-brutalist style, bold raw typography with high-contrast color blocking and thick borders.
Unpolished grid-breaking layout, stark black outlines, deliberately "unrefined" digital-native energy.

### 52. Neumorphism
Neumorphism style, soft extruded UI elements with subtle embossed light and shadow.
Monochromatic pastel palette, low contrast, smooth matte surfaces mimicking soft plastic.

### 53. Skeuomorphism
Skeuomorphic style, realistic textures mimicking physical materials like leather, wood, or metal.
Detailed shadows and highlights, tactile real-world object references, richly rendered surfaces.

### 54. Pop Art
Pop Art style, bold flat colors with comic-inspired Ben-Day dot patterns.
High-contrast outlines, saturated primary palette, playful mass-culture iconography.

### 55. Risograph / Riso Print
Risograph print style, layered flat colors with visible ink grain and slight misregistration.
Limited spot-color palette, textured paper feel, DIY zine-inspired printmaking charm.

### 56. Low Poly
Low poly style, faceted 3D geometry built from visible flat triangular planes.
Simplified stylized forms, gradient shading per facet, crystalline abstract 3D look.

### 57. Dopamine Design
Dopamine design style, ultra-saturated joyful colors with playful oversized shapes.
Bouncy rounded typography, energetic gradients, maximalist feel-good digital aesthetic.

### 58. Scandinavian Minimalism
Scandinavian minimalist style, light neutral palette with natural wood and linen textures.
Airy uncluttered composition, soft daylight tones, calm functional hygge simplicity.

### 59. Wabi-Sabi
Wabi-sabi style, embraces imperfection with organic, weathered, asymmetrical forms.
Muted earthy palette, natural textures, quiet understated beauty in rawness.

### 60. Steampunk
Steampunk style, Victorian-industrial aesthetic with brass gears and mechanical details.
Sepia and copper palette, ornate metalwork, retro-futuristic engineering craftsmanship.

### 61. Cel-Shaded / Anime Style
Cel-shaded style, flat color fills with hard-edged graphic shadows like animation cels.
Bold black outlines, saturated flat palette, stylized anime-inspired character rendering.

### 62. Glitch Art
Glitch art style, digital distortion with pixel sorting and RGB channel splitting.
Fragmented broken imagery, scan-line artifacts, chaotic corrupted-data aesthetic.

### 63. Voxel Art
Voxel art style, 3D objects built from cubic pixel blocks like digital building blocks.
Blocky stylized geometry, flat-shaded cube faces, charming toy-like miniature worlds.
